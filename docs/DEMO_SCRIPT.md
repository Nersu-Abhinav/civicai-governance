# 2–3 Minute Demo Script

## Opening — 15 seconds

> "Citizens have problems. Governments have data. The missing layer is intelligence that can turn thousands of unstructured citizen voices into a clear, explainable action queue. This is CivicAI Governance — JanMitra."

## Problem — 20 seconds

Show the citizen intake screen.

> "A citizen may report a pothole in English, Hindi, Telugu, or through voice and a photo. Today, these inputs can be fragmented and difficult to prioritize."

## Golden path — 60 seconds

1. Enter a realistic citizen report.
2. Add a ward/location.
3. Click **Analyze with CivicAI**.
4. Show the generated category, summary, department and priority.
5. Explain the recommendation using the visible priority rationale.

> "The AI layer extracts the intent, summarizes the request, identifies the service area, and recommends where it should go. The recommendation is explainable and remains subject to human review."

## Governance dashboard — 35 seconds

Show the priority queue and service-area panels.

> "Instead of seeing hundreds of isolated complaints, an official can see patterns: where high-priority issues are accumulating, which departments have the largest queues, and which problems are recurring."

## Impact — 25 seconds

> "The important shift is from complaint processing to community intelligence. A cluster of similar reports becomes evidence for an intervention. The system can help officials listen at scale without removing human accountability."

## Close — 15 seconds

> "CivicAI is designed as an AI layer for digital public infrastructure: multilingual, multimodal, explainable, privacy-aware, and deployable on Google Cloud. We are not automating government decisions. We are giving people and public servants a better way to understand each other."

## Demo tips

- Use a short, realistic report rather than a long paragraph.
- Demonstrate a Telugu or Hindi example if the live AI integration is ready.
- Show the dashboard immediately after the analysis.
- Never use real citizen personal information in the demo.
- Keep the prototype fast; explain architecture only after demonstrating the user value.
